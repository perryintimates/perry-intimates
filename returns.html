Returns & Refunds

Important information about return requests and order concerns.

At Perry Intimates, we want customers to have clear information when shopping with us. Please review the policy below and contact us if you have a concern about your order.

01. Contact Us About an Order

If you have a concern about an item you received, contact us directly with your order details and a clear explanation of the issue. We will communicate with you about the available next steps.

02. Eligibility for Returns

Return eligibility and any applicable conditions must be confirmed with Perry Intimates before an item is sent back. Please contact us before arranging a return.

03. Hygiene-Sensitive Products

Some intimate products may be subject to specific hygiene-related restrictions. Contact us before purchasing if you need clarification about the return conditions for a particular item.

04. Refunds and Exchanges

Refund and exchange eligibility, processing arrangements and any applicable conditions must be discussed with us before a return or exchange is arranged. A refund or exchange should not be assumed until the request has been reviewed and confirmed.

05. Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive an item that appears damaged or differs from what you ordered, contact us with your order details and information about the issue so we can review your concern.

06. Contact Us

For questions about returns, refunds or exchanges, please contact Perry Intimates directly before sending any item back.
