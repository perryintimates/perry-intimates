Shipping & Delivery

Everything you need to know about receiving your Perry Intimates order.

At Perry Intimates, we want your shopping experience to be straightforward, discreet and convenient. Please read this policy before placing an order.

01. Order Confirmation

Orders are subject to confirmation. Please ensure that the contact information and delivery details you provide are accurate so that we can communicate with you about your order.

02. Delivery Coverage

Delivery arrangements depend on your destination and the availability of suitable delivery services. Contact us to confirm whether delivery is available to your location before completing your order.

03. Delivery Fees

Delivery charges depend on the destination and the delivery service required. Any applicable delivery fee will be communicated and agreed with you before payment is completed.

04. Delivery Time

Delivery time may vary depending on your location, the delivery provider and other circumstances affecting transit. Please contact us for an estimated delivery timeframe before placing your order.

05. Accurate Delivery Information

Customers are responsible for providing a correct phone number, delivery address and any directions needed to complete delivery. Incorrect or incomplete information may cause delays or additional delivery arrangements.

06. Delivery Issues

If your order is delayed or you experience a delivery problem, please contact us with your order details so we can help clarify the status and available next steps.

07. Need Help?

If you have questions about delivery availability, charges or your order, please contact Perry Intimates before placing your order.
