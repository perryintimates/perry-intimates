Privacy Policy

How Perry Intimates may collect, use and protect information you share with us.

Perry Intimates respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be handled when you browse our website, contact us or make an enquiry about our products.

01. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may receive information such as:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • Delivery information you provide when arranging an order.
  • Order enquiries, messages and customer service communications.
  • Information your browser or device makes available when visiting a website, such as basic technical data.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is genuinely necessary for your enquiry or order.

02. How We Use Information

Information provided to us may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and customer service requests.
  • Confirm and coordinate orders and deliveries.
  • Communicate with you about an order or an issue you raise.
  • Maintain and improve our website and customer experience.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

03. Confidentiality

We understand that the nature of intimate products makes discretion important. We aim to handle customer information respectfully and limit its use to legitimate business, service and legal purposes.

Delivery providers or service partners may need relevant details to fulfil a delivery or provide a service.

04. Sharing Information

We do not intend to sell customers' personal information. Information may be shared where reasonably necessary to fulfil an order, operate website features, provide requested services or comply with the law.

05. Payments

Payment arrangements may involve third-party payment providers. Where a third-party provider processes a payment, its own terms and privacy practices may apply. Please review the provider's information before submitting payment details.

06. Cookies and Website Technology

Website features may use browser storage or similar technology to remember information needed for functionality, such as items added to a shopping cart. You can manage browser storage through your device or browser settings, although some website features may then work differently.

07. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to handle information carefully. However, no website, online service or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for customer service, order records, legitimate business purposes or legal requirements.

08. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have shared with us or to request a correction where appropriate. Requests will be considered in accordance with applicable law and any legitimate record-keeping obligations.

09. Third-Party Websites

Our website may link to services or social media platforms operated by other organisations. We do not control their websites or privacy practices. Please review their policies when using those services.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when our website, business practices or legal obligations change. The version published on this page will reflect the current wording of the policy.
